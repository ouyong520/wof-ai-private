## Zero-click vs visible product

The intended normal Alpha V1 behavior remains zero-click when semantic identity is safely unique.

However, the product must not remain invisible while waiting for perfect zero-click authority. The existing safety contract already permits at most one click fallback after a real automatic acquisition attempt fails closed.

Therefore the mainline candidate is acceptable for owner-visible product validation when:

- automatic semantic acquisition is attempted first;
- safe unique evidence binds with `ownerClickCount=0`;
- if automatic acquisition fails safely, one click on the actual in-game P1 head may seed the same production tracker;
- either path reaches the same visible production top-of-head overlay behavior;
- no manual calibration, projection-model guessing, body/feet/air clicking, or diagnostic-only overlay is required.

This does **not** permit claiming normal zero-click readiness unless W4/W5 authority proves it. It only prevents the whole visible Alpha V1 product from being withheld indefinitely behind that final automation requirement.
